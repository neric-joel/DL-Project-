Flags come from the verifier, a gradient-boosted model that predicts the clinically weighted error of each word and of each gap between words (numbers and negations ×10, medical terms ×5, other words ×1, function words ×0.2). It was trained on Eka calibration and validation clips and the 10 PriMock57 recalibration consultations, so consultations marked used in training are not a fair test.
